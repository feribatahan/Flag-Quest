How to play

  1. Choose a region or Expert mode.
  2. Look at the flag and select one of four country names.
  3. Correct answer = 1 point.
  4. In Challenge mode, answer before the 10-second timer ends.
  5. Finish the round, save your name, and try to beat your best score.

Each round has 10 questions. Questions are shuffled every time you play.
